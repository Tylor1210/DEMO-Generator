# Font pairs (L3)

Curated Google Fonts pairings. Pick the `id` that fits the brand's voice and the fonts found in `signals.json`. If the brand's own font is a Google Font, prefer a pair that contains it. Use the id exactly.

| id | display | body | Feel | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| poster | Anton | Inter | loud, condensed, uppercase | sodas, energy drinks, streetwear-adjacent snacks (Culture Pop uses this) |
| rounded | Bricolage Grotesque | Inter | friendly, chunky, modern | playful skincare, kids, fun snacks (Bubble uses this) |
| editorial | DM Serif Display | DM Sans | calm, premium, editorial | clean beauty, wellness, premium pantry |
| grotesk | Space Grotesk | IBM Plex Sans | precise, technical | supplements, performance, science-led brands |
| heritage | Fraunces | Work Sans | warm, crafted, a little old-school | coffee, bakeries, spirits, sauces with a story |
| bold-sans | Archivo Black | Archivo | heavy, confident, graphic | hot sauce, chili crisp, bold food brands |
| geometric | Syne | Manrope | artsy, distinctive | design-led brands, candles, small-batch goods |
| clean | Plus Jakarta Sans | Plus Jakarta Sans | neutral, crisp | when nothing else fits; minimal brands |

The template loads only the weights it needs: display 400 and 800, body 400 and 600.
