# Voice (L3)

All generated copy must sound like a sharp human copywriter who read the brand's site, not like a model.

## Do
- Short sentences. Plain words. Concrete nouns.
- Reuse the brand's own phrases and product names.
- Match the brand's register: playful brands get playful lines, clinical brands get calm ones.
- Headlines: 3 to 7 words. Giant outro type: 2 or 3 words, uppercase.
- Product names: keep the brand's words but write them in normal title case, even when the site styles them in all caps ("GRAPE" becomes "Grape", "STRAWBERRY & rhubarb" becomes "Strawberry & Rhubarb").
- Prices in copy read like a shop shows them: "$29.99", not "29.99 USD".
- CTAs are verbs: "Shop the lineup", "Find a store", "Build your routine".

## Don't
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- No "elevate", "unleash", "delve", "seamless", "journey", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "curated", "crafted with care", "look no further", "whether you're".
- No rhetorical triplets ("Bold. Fresh. Alive.") unless the brand writes that way.
- No exclamation marks unless the brand uses them.
- No claims the brand doesn't make. No superlatives ("best", "#1") unless quoted.
- No emoji.

## Shape of the hero
- `kicker`: product category in the brand's words ("Probiotic soda").
- `headline`: the single idea the brand stands for, as a promise.
- `sub`: one sentence of concrete facts from the site.
